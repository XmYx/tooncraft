- 2026-11-24: A second view and a navigator: the Second View panel shows the same shot with its own pan and zoom in four looks (Shot, Drawing, Camera, Side); the camera look films the frame off the UI thread, and the side look moves a layer in depth by drag or by Up and Down. The Navigator panel shows the whole picture with the main view's frame and moves the main view by click or by the arrow keys. `--second-view <look>` opens it.
