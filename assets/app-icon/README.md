# App icon

`tooncraft.svg` is the draft master (flip-book sheets with a bouncing ball), in the suite style:
Ink `#0b0b0c`, Paper `#f2f1ee` and the app colour `#e8604a`, a 512×512 tile with `rx=112`, art clipped
at 6/500/106. The hicolor PNGs, `tooncraft-1024.png`, `tooncraft.ico` and `tooncraft.icns` are rendered from
it by `cargo xtask icons` (craft-svg) and committed, so packaging needs no tools.
